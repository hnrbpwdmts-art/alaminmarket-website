Halal Turkey in Torrance, CA

Frozen whole halal turkeys for Thanksgiving and family dinners. $2.99/lb, about 12–14 lb each.

Turkey orders for this year are closed. Thank you! Call us to ask about next season.

Order a halal turkey → Call (310) 538-1188

Price and size

  • $2.99 per pound, sold by actual weight.
  • Average weight 12–14 lb, so most turkeys cost about $36–$42.
  • Comes frozen. Thaw it in the fridge before cooking; the USDA suggests about 24 hours for every 4–5 lb, so plan about 3 days for a 12–14 lb turkey.
  • 100% halal, from certified halal suppliers.

How to get yours

  1. Order online: choose how many turkeys and your pickup or delivery day (through Nov 25).
  2. We text you to confirm your order, exact total, and pickup time.
  3. Pick up at the store, or choose local delivery.

No payment when you order online. Pay at pickup or by Zelle; delivery orders are paid before they go out.

Planning for Thanksgiving?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26. Turkeys can be ordered for pickup or delivery through Wednesday, November 25; order a few days ahead so we can set yours aside. Questions? Call (310) 538-1188 or WhatsApp (424) 527-3540.
