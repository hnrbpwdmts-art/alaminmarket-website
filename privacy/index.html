Privacy notice

Last updated November 12, 2026

This notice explains what information Super Al-Amin Halal Market ("we") collects when you order through our website, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your order: the items, cuts, amounts, packing, notes, estimated total, and the pickup or delivery day and time you choose.
  • Contact details: your name and phone number, and your address if you choose delivery.
  • Your text preference: whether you agreed to receive deals and new-arrival texts.
  • Basic visit details: whether you used a phone or computer, how you sent the order (WhatsApp or text), and the website that sent you to us (for example, Google or Instagram).

We save this when you press Send by WhatsApp or Send by text. Your order message itself is sent through WhatsApp or your phone's messaging app, which have their own privacy policies.

How we use it

  • To prepare your order and contact you about it, including your ready time, final total, and payment link.
  • To understand what our customers buy, such as popular cuts and busy days, so we can stock and staff better.
  • To send you deals and new-arrival texts, only if you checked the box to agree.

Texts about deals

Marketing texts are optional and never needed to place an order. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, or ask us in the store.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We store order records with Google (Google Sheets) and use WhatsApp and phone carriers to message you. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for up to two years, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we have about you. California residents have these rights under California law. To ask, message us on WhatsApp, call us, or visit the store.
