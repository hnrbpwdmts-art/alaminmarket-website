Text Message Terms

Super Al-Amin Halal Market deals by text. Last updated November 20, 2026.

The program

Super Al-Amin Halal Market ("we") sends promotional text messages about meat specials, new arrivals and holiday offers to people who sign up at alaminmarket.com/deals, or who tick the optional box when ordering at alaminmarket.com/order.

How often

Message frequency varies, usually 2–4 messages per month.

Cost

We don't charge for these texts. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed, and no more after that. To join again, reply START or sign up again on our website.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, call us at (310) 538-1188, or visit the store at 18220 Prairie Ave, Torrance, CA 90504.

Consent

Signing up is optional and is not a condition of any purchase. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We never sell your phone number, and we do not share your phone number or text sign-up with other companies for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
